Naaval Carrier App

Install the driver app on iPhone.

Open this page in Safari on the iPhone, then add the carrier workspace to the Home Screen as a work app.

  1. Tap Open Carrier App.
  2. In Safari, tap the Share icon.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Name it Naaval Carrier and confirm.
